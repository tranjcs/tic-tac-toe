Privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This page explains what information the Tic-Tac-Toe game at ttt.code-brew.app collects and how it's used. There are no accounts and we never ask for your name, email or any other contact details.

Playing the game

To run a game, the server gives each browser tab a name like "Player 3" and keeps the current board, the matchup and the waiting line in memory. Nothing is saved to a database, and it's gone when you leave or the server restarts. Your tab remembers its player name in the browser's session storage so you keep it if you reconnect; it's deleted when you close the tab.

The game is hosted on Google Cloud Run. Like most web hosts, it keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, browser and time) for operating and securing the service.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service from Google, to understand how the game is used and to improve it.

Google Analytics doesn't load at all, so nothing is sent to Google, if:

  • you appear to be in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (we check your device's time zone, without looking up your IP address); or
  • your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal.

Otherwise, it collects:

  • pages viewed, the site that referred you, and visit length;
  • device type, operating system, browser, language and screen resolution;
  • approximate location (country and region), worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics doesn't log or store IP addresses;
  • how you play: moves (for example center, corner or edge), wins, losses and draws, how long games take, how long you waited for an opponent, and games that were left before the end.

Google Analytics doesn't receive your player name or anything that identifies you directly. We don't use it for advertising, and advertising features and ad personalization are turned off.

Cookies

Where Google Analytics loads, it sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*, kept for up to 2 years) to tell visits and returning visitors apart. If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland but your time zone says otherwise (for example while traveling), Google detects your region and sets no cookies.

Google's use of the data

Google processes this data on our behalf. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Opting out

Turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser and Google Analytics won't load. You can also block it with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, a content blocker, or your browser's cookie settings. The game works the same either way.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For questions, open an issue at github.com/tranjcs/tic-tac-toe/issues.
